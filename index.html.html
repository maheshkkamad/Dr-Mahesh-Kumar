About

Engineering ideas for healthcare applications

My work combines device-level modelling, experimental design thinking, and computational intelligence to explore practical biomedical and embedded-system solutions.

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of ICT at the School of Technology, Pandit Deendayal Energy University (PDEU), Gandhinagar. My research focuses on dielectrophoresis (DEP)-based microfluidics, MEMS, biosensors, and machine-learning-assisted optimization of healthcare devices.

I earned my PhD from Manipal University Jaipur, where my work focused on the design and optimization of DEP microfluidic cell-sorter devices for separating healthy and malignant cells. I completed my postgraduate studies at IIT Kharagpur. Before joining PDEU, I taught at Graphic Era (Deemed to be University), Dehradun, and Maharshi Parshuram College of Engineering, Ratnagiri.

My broader interests include translating computational models into accessible sensing platforms and embedded healthcare instruments.

Research

Research themes

Interdisciplinary work spanning micro-scale device physics, data-driven design, and embedded healthcare technologies.

RESEARCH AREA / 01

DEP-based microfluidic cell separation

Modelling and optimization of microfluidic devices that exploit dielectrophoretic response to support the separation of healthy and malignant cells.

COMSOL · FEM · Cell sorting
RESEARCH AREA / 02

AI-assisted device optimization

Surrogate models and multi-objective optimization to explore device operating conditions, performance trade-offs, and candidate design points.

ANFIS · NSGA-II · Explainable AI
RESEARCH AREA / 03

MEMS and biomedical sensors

Device design and analysis for sensing and actuation, with an interest in compact platforms for biomedical and point-of-care applications.

MEMS · Sensors · Actuators
RESEARCH AREA / 04

Impedance sensing and biosensing

Sensor concepts and readout approaches for detecting biological signals and supporting healthcare-oriented measurement systems.

Impedance · Biosensors
RESEARCH AREA / 05

Embedded intelligence and IoT

Microcontroller-based systems, sensor integration, connectivity, and edge intelligence for practical instrumentation and monitoring.

Embedded systems · IoT
RESEARCH AREA / 06

Disposable diagnostic platforms

Exploring the integration of microdevices, sensors, and intelligent instruments to support accessible diagnostic workflows.

Lab-on-chip · Instrumentation
